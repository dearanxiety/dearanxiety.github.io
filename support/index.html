Free access

Every feature is free. You can create cats, add check-ins, attach photos, record voice notes, review patterns, export your data, and rescue cats without a subscription or in-app purchase.

Photos and voice notes

The iOS photo picker lets you choose individual photos without giving DearAnxiety access to your whole library. If microphone permission was declined, open the iPhone Settings app, choose DearAnxiety, and allow Microphone access.

Your local data

Settings in the app includes controls to export your local data or delete it.

Urgent support

DearAnxiety does not provide medical, crisis, or emergency support. Contact local emergency services or a qualified professional when immediate help is needed.
